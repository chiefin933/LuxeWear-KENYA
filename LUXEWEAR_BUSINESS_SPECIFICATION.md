## 1. Business Identity & Brand Definition

### 1.1 Brand Vision & Positioning
**LuxeWear Kenya** is a premium contemporary fashion house offering high-quality, elegant, and sophisticated apparel tailored for modern Kenyan urban professionals. The brand blends luxury aesthetics, clean silhouettes, and premium fabrics with practical, seamless local checkout (M-Pesa Express) and swift Nairobi delivery.

### 1.2 Brand Pillars
1. **Uncompromised Quality**: Premium fabrics (silk satin, linen blends, structured crepe, organic cotton) meticulously crafted for longevity and elegance.
2. **Effortless Sophistication**: Clean designs suitable for high-level business meetings, evening galas, weekend brunches, and luxury leisure.
3. **Local Trust & Seamlessness**: Lightning-fast mobile checkout, instant M-Pesa verification, transparent delivery updates, and responsive customer care.
4. **Exclusivity**: Limited-run seasonal collections to maintain exclusivity and prevent mass production feel.

### 1.3 Design System & Visual Identity Guidelines
- **Palette**:
  - **Primary Background**: Warm Ivory `#FDFBF7` / Rich Charcoal `#121212` (Dark mode)
  - **Surface Neutral**: Soft Sand `#F5F2ED` / Charcoal Surface `#1E1E1E`
  - **Primary Text**: Deep Obsidian `#1A1A1A`
  - **Muted Text**: Slate Neutral `#666666`
  - **Brand Accent / Highlight**: Warm Muted Gold `#C59B74`
  - **Border Color**: Subtle Linen `#E5E0D8`
  - **Status Colors**: Success (Emerald `#2E7D32`), Warning (Amber `#ED6C02`), Error (Ruby `#D32F2F`)
- **Typography**:
  - **Headings**: *Playfair Display* / *Cormorant Garamond* (Serif, Elegant, Authoritative)
  - **Body & UI**: *Inter* / *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Sans-serif, Crisp, Legible)
- **Tone of Voice**: Sophisticated, polite, confident, inspiring, and concise.

## 4. Pricing, Currency & Promotion Rules

1. **Currency**: All store prices are strictly displayed and processed in **Kenyan Shillings (KES)**.
2. **Taxation**: Prices are inclusive of 16% Value Added Tax (VAT) where applicable.
3. **Discount & Sale Rules**:
   - Promotional codes (`PROMO_CODE`) apply either flat KES discounts or percentage discounts (e.g., `LUXE10` for 10% off first order).
   - Backend MUST recalculate subtotal, discount, delivery fee, and final payable amount server-side.
   - Frontend price display is strictly informational; backend computed amount is authoritative.

---

## 5. Inventory & Stock Rules

1. **Variant Level Tracking**: Inventory is tracked at the exact SKU + Size + Color variant level (`ProductVariant.stock_quantity`).
2. **Cart Reservation Lock**:
   - When a customer enters checkout and initiates payment, items are locked for **15 minutes**.
   - If payment fails or times out, the lock expires and stock returns to the available pool.
3. **Low Stock Threshold**:
   - Stock level `<= 3` triggers a "Low Stock — Only X left" badge on the product detail page and an automatic admin alert.
4. **Out of Stock Rules**:
   - Out of stock variants cannot be added to cart.
   - Customers can request a "Notify me when back in stock" via WhatsApp/Email.

### 6.1 Zones & Rates

  - Areas covered: Kilimani, Westlands, Lavington, Karen, Runda, Parklands, CBD, Kileleshwa, South C, Lang'ata.

  - Next Day Delivery: **KES 400**

### 6.2 Fulfillment Status Lifecycle
```
PENDING → PAID → PROCESSING (Packing) → DISPATCHED (With Rider/Courier) → DELIVERED
                                      └→ CANCELLED / REFUNDED
```

## 7. Payment Rules & M-Pesa Integration

### 7.1 Supported Payment Methods
1. **M-Pesa Express (STK Push)** [PRIMARY]
2. **M-Pesa Paybill / Till Number (Manual Code Entry Fallback)**
3. **Credit / Debit Card (Visa, Mastercard via Paystack/Flutterwave/I&M)** [SECONDARY]

### 7.2 Strict M-Pesa Rules
- **No Client Trust**: Client frontend NEVER sets `order.payment_status = 'PAID'`.
- **STK Push Workflow**:
  1. Customer inputs phone number (`2547XXXXXXXX`).
  2. Backend triggers M-Pesa Daraja API STK Push (`LIPA_NA_MPESA_ONLINE`).
  3. M-Pesa sends PIN prompt to customer's mobile device.
  4. M-Pesa sends async callback to backend endpoint `/api/v1/payments/mpesa/callback`.
  5. Backend verifies payload signature, transaction code (`MpesaReceiptNumber`), and amount matched against order.
  6. Backend updates order state to `PAID`, deducts stock, creates audit record, and triggers receipt notification via SMS/Email/WhatsApp.

## 8. Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. **Return Window**: Customers may return items within **7 days** of delivery.
2. **Item Condition**: Items must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, with original LuxeWear tags attached and in original packaging.
3. **Refund Method**:
   - Returns are refunded via **Store Credit / Digital Voucher** or **Direct M-Pesa Reversal** (for defective items).
   - Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to LuxeWear error (wrong item/defective item).
4. **Cancellation**: Orders can be cancelled penalty-free prior to `DISPATCHED` status.

## 11. Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Lifecycle

### 11.1 Customer Lifecycle Tiers
1. **Anonymous Visitor**: Browsing website, no identified contact details.
2. **Lead / Prospect**: Added item to cart, initiated checkout, or engaged with WhatsApp bot without purchase.
3. **First-Time Buyer**: Has 1 completed `PAID` order.
4. **Repeat Customer**: Has 2–4 completed orders.
5. **VIP Luminary**: Lifetime Spend >= KES 50,000 OR 5+ completed orders. Entitled to free express delivery, early access to new drops, and personal fashion concierge service.
6. **At-Risk / Inactive**: No purchases in 90+ days. Targeted with re-engagement offers.

## 12. Automation & AI Guardrails (n8n + WhatsApp)

### 12.1 Authorized AI Agent Capabilities
- **Product Search & Recommendations**: Search inventory based on customer query (e.g., "Do you have red evening dresses under 8000?").
- **Order Status Lookup**: Provide order status updates when customer provides phone number or Order ID.
- **Size & Fit Advice**: Provide sizing recommendations based on size chart.
- **FAQ Answering**: Delivery rates, return policy, store location.

### 12.2 Strict AI Boundaries & Guardrails
- 🛑 **NO Stock Hallucination**: AI must ONLY state stock levels returned directly by the backend database API.
- 🛑 **NO Price Alterations**: AI cannot offer custom discounts or alter product prices.
- 🛑 **NO Payment Approval**: AI cannot mark an order as paid or generate fake receipts.
- 🛑 **Human Escalation**: AI MUST immediately route to a human agent when:
  - Customer requests refund or cancellation.
  - Customer reports damaged or missing item.
  - Sentiment analysis detects severe customer frustration/anger.
